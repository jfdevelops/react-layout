Add `variant="toolbar"` to `ReactLayoutDevtools`: a bar docked to the bottom of the viewport with live layout counts, the current resource and state, a state override and the outline toggle. It expands into a resizable drawer with the full devtools, and its height is saved. The page reserves matching space through `--react-layout-devtools-toolbar-height`. Alt+Shift+L toggles the drawer and Alt+Shift+H hides the bar. Users can switch variants from either UI, and the choice persists.
